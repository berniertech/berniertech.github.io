# Privacy Policy for Bernier Text-Data-Save Browser

Effective Date: 2026

BernierTech ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bernier Text-Data-Save Browser. This policy explains our approach to your privacy and data.

## 1. Data Collection
The Bernier Text-Data-Save Browser is designed with a **privacy-first** approach. 
- **No Personal Data**: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data.
- **Local Storage**: Site permissions and offline pages are stored locally on your device only.
- **Session Cleanup**: The app is configured to clear cookies and web cache automatically when closed.

## 2. Bandwidth Firewall
The app block images, videos, and trackers locally on your device to save bandwidth and improve privacy. No third-party servers process your requests.

## 3. Third-Party Websites
When you visit websites using this browser, those sites may have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party websites.
