Refund Policy

Refund terms for software, SaaS and digital FinOps services.

This page explains when refunds may be granted, and why digital delivery is generally non-refundable once access has begun.

Refund contact

  • support@berniertech.com
  • Instant electronic delivery
  • Refunds only where required by law or delivery failure
Policy summary

Digital products and services are delivered immediately.

Bernier Tech sells software, SaaS subscriptions and professional FinOps advisory services. Because access is delivered electronically and value may be received immediately, refunds are limited once delivery or use begins.

Non-refundable delivery

When refunds are not available.

  • Software downloads, license keys, account access and SaaS activations are non-refundable once issued.
  • Professional consultancy, reports and advisory services are non-refundable after work begins or material is delivered.
  • Use of the software, implementation of recommendations or receipt of service is considered acceptance of delivery.
Limited refund rights

When a refund may be considered.

  • If a product or service is materially different from what was described at the time of purchase.
  • If an order is duplicated or processed incorrectly.
  • If access cannot be delivered due to a failure on our part.
  • Any statutory consumer rights still apply where required by law in the UK, EU or other jurisdiction.
Subscription cancellation

Renewals and cancellations.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the next billing date. Cancellation requests should be sent to support@berniertech.com or made through the checkout platform.

Refunds are not normally available for subscription periods that have already been provided, except when required by applicable law or when the third-party checkout platform explicitly permits a refund.

Delivery completed

What constitutes delivery.

Delivery occurs when software access is granted, a license key or activation credentials are provided, or a service engagement is confirmed. At that point, the product or service is considered delivered and the purchase becomes final unless a legal refund right applies.

Platform terms

Third-party payment platforms.

Payments are processed by third-party platforms such as Lemon Squeezy, Stripe or Fastspring. Their checkout policies and dispute resolution processes may also apply, and may affect how refunds or chargebacks are handled.

Consumer rights

UK and EU statutory rights.

For consumers located in the UK or EU, your statutory rights under consumer protection and digital contract laws are not affected by this policy. If local law provides additional refund rights, those rights apply in addition to the terms on this page.

Requesting a refund

How to contact us.

For refund requests, email support@berniertech.com with your order details and the reason for your request. We will review each request and respond as soon as practicable.
