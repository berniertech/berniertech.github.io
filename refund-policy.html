Refund Policy

30-Day Money Back Guarantee

We offer a 30-day money back guarantee for all digital products and software purchased through this website. If you're not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.

To request a refund, please contact us at support@berniertech.com with your order details. Refunds will be processed through our payment processor according to their policies.

Subscription Services

Subscription Cancellation and Refunds

For subscription services, you can cancel at any time. Refunds for unused portions of subscription periods will be processed according to our payment processor's terms.

Platform terms

Payments are processed by third-party platforms such as Lemon Squeezy, Stripe or Fastspring. Their checkout policies and dispute resolution processes may also apply, and may affect how refunds or chargebacks are handled.

Consumer rights

UK and EU statutory rights.

For consumers located in the UK or EU, your statutory rights under consumer protection and digital contract laws are not affected by this policy. If local law provides additional refund rights, those rights apply in addition to the terms on this page.

Requesting a refund

How to contact us.

For refund requests, email support@berniertech.com with your order details and the reason for your request. We will review each request and respond as soon as practicable.
